\section{Related work}

\Textcite{IsomottonenCochez2014} document Git learning challenges from
course experience and recommend visual, pointer-based teaching of
branches; \textcite{DeRossoJackson2016Gitless} locate part of the
difficulty in Git's concept design and demonstrate a redesign (Gitless);
the GitKit project catalogues misconceptions in basic workflows across
institutions \autocite{Postner2026GitMisconceptions}; and the Shell Tutor
tutors Git through the shell \autocite{Winder2024ShellTutor}.
Our contribution is the variation-theory analysis on top of this
literature: from documented misconceptions to critical aspects to
teachable patterns of variation, as in the companion papers
\autocite{VTProgMisconceptions,VTDebug,VTTerminal}.
